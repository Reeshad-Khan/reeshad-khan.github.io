Reeshad Khan Ph.D.

I work on efficient AI for autonomous systems, with a focus on BEV perception, multisensor fusion, sensing-aware learning, and real-time deployment under noisy and resource-constrained settings.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Arkansas, where my dissertation focused on efficient deep neural networks for autonomous perception. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, robust perception, and deployable machine learning systems.

Research

My work aims to make autonomous perception systems more efficient, robust, and deployable in real-world settings. I am particularly interested in models that preserve strong performance while respecting sensing, latency, and compute constraints.

Core Topics

  • 3D perception, tracking, motion prediction, and planning in BEV
  • Radar-vision fusion and uncertainty-aware perception
  • Robust learning under noisy or resource-constrained sensing
  • Efficient models for GPU and embedded deployment

Tools and Systems

  • PyTorch, CUDA, Linux, Docker, and MLOps workflows
  • CARLA, SUMO, and simulation-driven evaluation
  • Experiment design, benchmarking, and visualization pipelines
  • RAW-to-task sensing and restoration-aware learning
